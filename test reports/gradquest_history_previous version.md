## Strategic Observations

1.  **Morale Management:** The advisor's supportive trait ("Take a week off") was crucial for recovering from low morale events like "Did they make a mistake admitting you?" and "Major Revision." Using the `Take a Break` action proactively also helped.
2.  **Publication Pipeline:** All three papers required a **Major Revision** before acceptance. This suggests a high difficulty setting or a common game mechanic, requiring an extra month of `Revise Paper` action for each.
3.  **Equipment Failure:** Equipment failure was a recurring event (Months 16, 32, 35) that blocked the `Validate Discovery` action. Attending a `Conference` or performing another action seemed to trigger the "Equipment Fixed" event.
4.  **Efficiency:** The total time of 56 months (4 years, 8 months) is a reasonable time for a PhD, demonstrating a successful and relatively efficient run despite the setbacks. The key was immediately starting the next paper's pipeline (`Work on Idea`) while the previous one was under review.
5.  **Network:** The network score of 35 was built entirely through `Conference` actions and the initial `Read Papers` action, which also contributed to the successful graduation.
