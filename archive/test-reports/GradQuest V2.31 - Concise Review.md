# GradQuest V2.31 - Concise Review

**Reviewer:** Manus  
**Date:** January 8, 2026  
**Game Version:** V2.31

---

## 1. Key Observations

Version 2.31 appears to be a minor update focused on small refinements rather than major new features. The core gameplay loop and the significant mechanics introduced in V2.30 are unchanged.

*   **NPC Name Change:** The labmate NPCs have been renamed from Elena/Jordan to **Marcus/Alex**. This is the most visible change in this version.
*   **Mechanical Stability:** The core mechanics, including the research pipeline, Morale system, and seasonal conferences, function as they did in V2.30. No new major actions or systems were observed during this initial review.
*   **UI Consistency:** The user interface, including the layout of actions and status panels, remains consistent with the previous version.

---

## 2. Suggestions for Future Improvements

Given that V2.31 is a minor revision, the suggestions from the V2.30 review remain highly relevant. Here are the most impactful ones to consider for future updates:

1.  **Implement Labmate Mechanics:** The renaming of the labmates (now Marcus and Alex) highlights the opportunity to give them a mechanical role. Introducing random events where they can help or hinder the player's progress would significantly increase the game's narrative depth and replayability.

2.  **Stabilize Action Button Layout:** The issue of action buttons shifting their positions when new, temporary actions appear still persists. Grouping actions by category (e.g., "Core Research," "Personal," "Opportunities") would create a more stable and user-friendly interface, especially for long-term players.

3.  **Enhance Visual Feedback:** A visual progress bar for the "Thesis" would provide a more satisfying sense of accomplishment than the current percentage text. This would make the ultimate goal of the game feel more tangible as the player progresses.

---

## 3. Final Verdict

**V2.31 is a stable, incremental update.** It maintains the excellent strategic depth introduced in V2.30. The focus for future development should now be on adding more narrative flavor through NPC interactions and improving the user experience with a more consistent UI layout.
