# GradQuest V2.17: Gameplay Feedback and Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary

GradQuest V2.17 introduces several significant and welcome additions that enhance the strategic depth and player agency compared to the previous V2.16 version. The most notable changes are the introduction of the **Alignment** mechanic and two new strategic actions: **Equipment Maintenance** and **Pre-Register Idea**. These features directly address some of the unpredictability and lack of control experienced in earlier versions, making the game a more refined and engaging PhD life simulator.

## 2. Analysis of New Mechanics in V2.17

The core strength of V2.17 lies in providing players with more tools to manage risk and build a more stable PhD journey.

### 2.1. Alignment System

The new **Alignment** stat tracks the player's relationship with their advisor more granularly than the simple "Happy/Neutral/Angry" status.

| Feature | Description | Impact on Gameplay |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stat Tracking** | Increases through successful Pitch Sessions. | Encourages regular advisor interaction beyond just research updates. |
| **Morale Decay** | Reduces monthly morale decay (-1 per 25 alignment). | Directly rewards players for maintaining a good advisor relationship, making morale management easier in the long run. |
| **Events** | Triggers advisor pep talks at high levels. | Provides positive feedback and likely morale/stat boosts for high alignment. |

**Observation:** The Alignment system is a fantastic addition. It transforms the Pitch Session from a simple information-gathering tool into a core strategic action for long-term stability. The explicit link between alignment and reduced morale decay is a clear and effective incentive for players to engage with this mechanic.

### 2.2. New Strategic Actions

The introduction of **Equipment Maintenance** and **Pre-Register Idea** gives the player proactive control over two major negative events: equipment failure and being scooped.

| Action | Cost/Effect | Strategic Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Equipment Maintenance** | Trades one month of research for 12 months of equipment stability. | **High.** In V2.16, equipment failure was a crippling random event that could only be fixed by using a valuable Conference slot. This new action allows players to proactively prevent the failure, making the early game more manageable and strategic. |
| **Pre-Register Idea** | Costs -5 network, prevents being scooped. | **Medium.** This is a clever trade-off. It allows players to protect a high-value idea, but at the cost of a resource (Network) that is crucial for end-game scenarios and collaboration. The cost is clear and the benefit is preventative. |

**Observation:** The Equipment Maintenance action is a direct and successful response to the frustration of the random "Broken Equipment" event in V2.16. It converts a purely random roadblock into a strategic decision, which is a significant improvement in game design.

### 2.3. Updated Strategic Exits

The help screen now provides more detail on the Master's Exit option:

*   **Master's Exit:** Requires 30 credits + 18 months.
*   **Early Exit → Career Pivot**
*   **Late Exit → Technical Specialist (+salary)**

**Observation:** Explicitly stating the requirements (30 credits + 18 months) for the Master's Exit is a great UI/UX improvement. It gives players a clear, measurable goal for a strategic exit, which is a realistic element of the PhD experience.

## 3. Suggestions for Further Improvement

### 3.1. Alignment and Advisor Feedback

While the Alignment stat is excellent, its current effect on the advisor's mood (e.g., dropping from "Happy" to "Neutral" after a failed research attempt) is still somewhat opaque.

*   **Suggestion:** Provide a more explicit link between the Alignment score and the success rate of research actions (e.g., "Work on Idea," "Develop Findings"). For instance, a high Alignment score could grant a small bonus to the success chance of research actions, making the stat feel more impactful on the core research loop.

### 3.2. Pre-Register Idea Cost/Benefit

The cost of -5 Network for Pre-Registering an Idea seems relatively low for preventing a potentially devastating "scooped" event.

*   **Suggestion:** Consider making the cost variable or adding a time component. For example, pre-registering could take one month of time (representing the bureaucratic process) in addition to the network cost. Alternatively, the network cost could scale with the value of the idea.

### 3.3. Quals Prep Clarity

In my playthrough, the Quals Prep went from 2/3 to "QUALS PASSED!" after the final prep session.

*   **Suggestion:** The final prep session should ideally occur in August (Month 12) to be ready for the September (Month 13) exam. The UI should clearly indicate that the final prep session is the one that triggers the exam success, and perhaps provide a small morale boost for completing the preparation on time.

## 4. Conclusion

GradQuest V2.17 is a substantial and well-executed update. The new mechanics—Alignment, Equipment Maintenance, and Pre-Register Idea—significantly improve the strategic depth and player control, successfully converting random frustrations into meaningful strategic choices. The game continues to be a highly engaging and realistic simulation of the PhD journey.
