# GradQuest V2.28 - QA & UX Analysis Report

## 1. Executive Summary
GradQuest V2.28 is a highly immersive, text-based PhD simulator that effectively captures the stress and strategic complexity of academic life. While the core mechanics are robust and the UI is clean, the current balancing leans heavily toward a punishing experience. In three distinct playthroughs (Balanced, Safe, and Risky), all resulted in failure due to morale depletion, primarily driven by negative RNG events like being "Scooped."

## 3. Mechanic Analysis
### RNG & Balance
- **Morale Penalty:** The morale system is the primary driver of difficulty. Negative events like "Scooped" (-10) and "Semester Chaos" (-3) create a downward spiral that is difficult to recover from.
- **Last-Minute Cram:** This mechanic is currently a "newbie trap." While presented as an emergency solution for Quals, the -25 morale penalty often leads to an immediate "Game Over" if the player is already struggling.
- **Event Frequency:** The frequency of "Scooped" events feels high (occurring twice in 18 months in one run), which can feel more like bad luck than a manageable challenge.

### Specializations
- **Experimentalist:** ✨ Protocol Reuse (saves time on findings), ⚠️ Equipment-dependent (RNG failures can stall research).
- **Theoretician:** ✨ Conceptual Breakthrough (faster discovery), ⚠️ Abstract results (harder to validate figures).
- **Computational:** ✨ Pipeline Automation (faster figure generation), ⚠️ Server-dependent (maintenance costs morale/time).

## 4. UX & UI Feedback
- **Clarity of State:** The dashboard is excellent. Morale, advisor mood, and research progress are always visible. The "Research Pipeline" visualization is a standout feature.
- **Feedback Loops:** The game provides clear text feedback for all actions. However, the "Previously" log is somewhat small and can be overlooked during rapid play.
- **Pain Points:** The transition from "Discovery" to "Figures" can feel slow, especially when combined with the pressure of upcoming Quals.

## 5. Bug Report & Technical Issues
- **Soft Locks:** No technical soft-locks were encountered. However, "Morale Spirals" act as a gameplay soft-lock where recovery becomes statistically improbable.
- **Technical Robustness:** Save/Load functionality and Seed generation are fully functional and reliable.
- **UI Scaling:** The game performs well in a standard browser environment, though some text-heavy dialogs (like Help) require significant scrolling.

## 6. Recommendations
1. **Adjust "Scooped" Frequency:** Reduce the probability of being scooped in the first 12 months to allow players to build a morale buffer.
2. **Rework "Last-Minute Cram":** Instead of a flat morale penalty, consider a "Debt" system where morale is reduced over the next 3 months, or allow it to succeed at a lower morale cost if "Network" is high.
3. **Buff "Take a Break":** Increase the morale recovery from standard breaks slightly to help players exit the "Morale Spiral."
